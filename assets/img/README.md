# Images

Drop the portrait photograph in here as `kat-portrait.jpg`.

The hero on `index.html` references it at its natural proportions
(1333 × 2000). If you replace it with a differently sized file, update the
`width` and `height` attributes on that `<img>` to match — they reserve the
space while the image loads so the page doesn't jump.

Save it at roughly 1333px wide. A JPEG at quality 80 is plenty; anything
larger just costs your visitors load time.
