Privacy policy

How this website handles the information you send through it.

What this website collects

The only information this website collects is what you type into the enquiry form: your name, your email address, the topic you select, and your message.

There is no analytics on this site, no tracking, and no advertising. Nothing is collected when you simply read a page.

Why, and on what basis

Your enquiry is used for one purpose: to reply to you and, if you go ahead, to arrange an initial appointment.

The lawful basis is legitimate interests, meaning responding to someone who has chosen to make contact. If your message describes your health, that is special category data, and the basis for holding it is that you have made it public to us by choosing to send it, for the purpose of seeking care.

The form asks you to keep your message brief and not to include detailed clinical information, precisely so that sensitive detail is discussed in session rather than sent through a web form.

Who else handles it

Your enquiry is not sold, and it is not shared with anyone beyond the services used to run this website and deliver your message to me.

Cookies

This website sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device.
