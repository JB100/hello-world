Privacy policy

How this website handles the information you send through it.

Before publishing: the bracketed items below need your details, and the retention period needs to be a length you will actually keep to. This is a starting point written to match what this website does — it is not legal advice, and it does not replace the separate privacy notice covering your therapy records.

Who this is from

This website is run by Kat Lech Psychotherapy. For anything relating to your information, contact [postal address or contact route].

Under UK data protection law the practice is the “controller” for the information described here, which means it decides how and why that information is used. [ICO registration number, if registered]

What this website collects

The only information this website collects is what you type into the enquiry form: your name, your email address, the topic you select, and your message.

There is no analytics on this site, no tracking, and no advertising. Nothing is collected when you simply read a page.

Why, and on what basis

Your enquiry is used for one purpose: to reply to you and, if you go ahead, to arrange an initial appointment.

The lawful basis is legitimate interests — responding to someone who has chosen to make contact. If your message describes your health, that is special category data, and the basis for holding it is that you have made it public to us by choosing to send it, for the purpose of seeking care.

The form asks you to keep your message brief and not to include detailed clinical information, precisely so that sensitive detail is discussed in session rather than sent through a web form.

Who else handles it

  • The enquiry form is delivered by Formspree, which passes your message on by email. Their privacy terms apply to that step.
  • The site is hosted by [hosting provider], whose servers handle the request when you load a page.
  • Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts. Your browser requests them from Google, which involves your IP address reaching Google's servers.

Your enquiry is not sold, and it is not shared with anyone beyond what is described here.

How long it is kept

Enquiries that do not lead to therapy are deleted after [period — choose something you will keep to].

If you become a client, your information moves into your clinical record, which is covered by a separate privacy notice given to you at the start of therapy and kept for the period required of accredited therapists.

Cookies

This website sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device.

Your rights

You can ask what information is held about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, ask that it be restricted, or object to it being held at all. To do any of these, get in touch using the details above.

If you are unhappy with how your information has been handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.
