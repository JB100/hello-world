A little about me

Choosing a therapist is a personal decision, so here is a little of what I am like away from the therapy room.

Outside of therapy, you'll find me outdoors with my family and our two dogs. I love going for walks, hiking and exploring new places.

I also enjoy the simple pleasure of a good cup of coffee in a quiet coffee shop, watching the world go by.

I'm happiest when I'm spending time with the people I love, discovering somewhere new or simply enjoying the little things in everyday life.
