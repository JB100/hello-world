Take the first step

Starting therapy can feel daunting, particularly if you've been struggling for a long time or aren't sure whether therapy is right for you.

You don't need to have everything worked out before getting in touch.

If you'd like to find out whether I could help, you can arrange an initial consultation to discuss what you're experiencing and what you're looking for from therapy.
